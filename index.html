Universal per-service workflow

  1. Discover: identify host reachability and TCP/UDP listeners with scope-aware scan rates.
  2. Fingerprint: identify service, product, version, TLS and protocol response; do not infer solely from port.
  3. Enumerate: use native clients and safe scripts; begin with unauthenticated metadata only.
  4. Authenticate: use owner-provided test accounts; validate least privilege and MFA without guessing passwords.
  5. Validate: use read-only requests or a dedicated test object; avoid production data and state changes.
  6. Evidence: save exact command, tool version, timestamp, sanitized output and affected asset.
  7. Report: explain exposure, prerequisites, business impact, remediation and retest result.
